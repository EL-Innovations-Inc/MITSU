The mother function

The forge

A neuron hands us one number u, but the formula wants three positive ones. So the signal takes the x seat through a softplus, s = softplus(u), and y, z become shape dials a, b. Subtracting the resting level F₀ = (ab+ba)/(a+b) parks the left tail at zero.

The block tower

The extended formula splits exactly: block(u) = MITSU(u) + (i+j+k)/(s+a+b) — nonlinearity, skip, and divisive normalization from one expression. Sum and mean carry take i, j, k from the three nearest units of the previous layer; three-seat gives one unit three pre-activations and lets them compete.

Trains on the dataset chosen in the lab above; the a, b dials act on every block live. The dashed line is the tower running in pure-relay mode: sum carry climbs by 3/(a+b) per layer — the 1.5^L runaway — mean carry decays by 1/(a+b), and three-seat drops to zero. The three-seat block subtracts its resting level (the cross-powers sit at 3 in silence), caps exponents at 6, and bounds the competition bases at 2·sigmoid(u) — the "sigmoids in the bases" guard. With unbounded softplus bases, two co-active strong channels really do go double-exponential and detonate deep towers; bounded bases keep the same s = 1 threshold at u = 0 while making runaway impossible.

With a = b = 1 the whole construction collapses to s(s−1)/(s+2) with s = softplus(u): smooth, a small negative dip of about −0.10 near u ≈ −0.57, asymptotically linear, gradient bounded — a homemade cousin of GELU and Mish. The sum y+z sets the species: 2 grows linearly, (1,2) grows like a power, exactly 1 saturates, below 1 inhibits.
